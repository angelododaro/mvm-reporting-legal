MVM Reporting Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Magic vs. Machine Inc. (“MVM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) uses the MVM Reporting Facebook app as an internal tool to manage and report on Meta advertising accounts that our clients have authorized us to access.

Information we access

The app may access ad account data such as campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, budgets, delivery, and performance metrics, along with the Facebook Page and Instagram identities used for those ads.

How we use this information

We use this information only to deliver our advertising services. We do not sell it. We share it only with MVM team members working on the relevant account and with Meta as needed to operate the ads.

Retention

We keep this information as long as needed to provide our services and meet recordkeeping obligations.

Access and deletion

To request access or deletion of data this app holds, email angelo@magicvsmachine.com.
